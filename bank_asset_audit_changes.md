## Thailand Credit Card Partnership Notes

These do not change bank-list IDs, but they matter if credit-card issuers or card brands are added separately:

| Bank / group | Credit-card relationship |
|---|---|
| UOB Thailand | UOB acquired Citi's Thailand consumer banking and credit-card business; UOB says it is the issuer of Citi-branded consumer products during the transition period. |
| Krungthai Bank | Krungthai Card Public Company Limited (KTC) is a separate listed payment/retail-lending company with Krungthai Bank as major shareholder. KTC cards should not be treated as a duplicate Krungthai Bank entry without a product decision. |
| SCB | SCB credit cards can be provided alongside CardX within SCBX; in 2026 SCB WEALTH and CardX launched a co-branded SCB WEALTH Credit Card by CardX. |
| Krungsri | Krungsri/Bank of Ayudhya has card and personal-loan subsidiaries such as General Card Services, which operates Central The 1 Card and Simple Visa Card. |
